Pick a date and a time together in Google Sheets™

Google Sheets™ only gives you a calendar. DateTime Picker adds the missing time picker, with any time zone, and inserts the result into your cells as ISO 8601 text. Free and open source.

Features

  • Date and time in one dialog, for one cell or a whole range
  • Inserts ISO 8601 text such as 2026-11-12T16:00:00.000Z, or a formatted date value
  • GMT+0 by default, with every other time zone available and the live offset shown
  • Optional conversion to GMT before inserting
  • Insert the current date and time in one click
  • Settings saved per spreadsheet

How to use

  1. Select the cells you want to fill.
  2. Open Extensions > DateTime Picker > Pick date and time.
  3. Pick the date, time and time zone, then click Insert.

FAQ

Does it read my data? Only what it needs. It reads the selected cell to prefill the picker with any existing date, and writes the value you pick into the cells you select. Nothing leaves your spreadsheet, and no AI is used. See the privacy policy.

Does it work on mobile? Add-ons are not supported in the Google Sheets™ mobile apps, so use it in a desktop browser.
