Common questions

Where is the menu? After installing, open a spreadsheet and go to Extensions > DateTime Picker. Reload the spreadsheet if it does not appear.

What format is inserted? ISO 8601 text, for example 2026-11-12T16:00:00.000Z for GMT+0, or 2026-11-12T21:30:00.000+05:30 for another time zone. You can switch to a formatted date value in Settings.

How do I change the default time zone? Open Extensions > DateTime Picker > Settings. The default is GMT+0.

How do I uninstall? Go to Extensions > Add-ons > Manage add-ons, find DateTime Picker and choose Uninstall.
