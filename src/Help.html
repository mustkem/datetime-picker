Pick date and time - select one or more cells, then open Extensions > DateTime Picker > Pick date and time. Choose the date, time and time zone together and click Insert.

Output - by default cells get ISO 8601 text such as 2026-11-12T16:00:00.000Z (GMT) or 2026-11-12T21:30:00.000+05:30. Switch to a formatted date value in Settings.

Convert to GMT - stores the GMT equivalent of the time you picked.

Free and open source: github.com/mustkem/datetime-picker
